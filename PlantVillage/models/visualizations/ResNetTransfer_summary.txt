层类型统计:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
层类型                       数量         参数量            
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Conv2d                    53              23,454,912
BatchNorm2d               53                  53,120
Bottleneck                16              23,498,496
Sequential                9               27,459,594
Linear                    3                1,182,986
BatchNorm1d               2                    1,536
ResNetTransfer            1               24,692,554
ResNet                    1               24,692,554

模型架构说明:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. 骨干网络: ResNet50 (ImageNet预训练权重)
   - 冻结所有卷积层和残差块
   - 仅训练自定义分类头
2. 特征提取: AdaptiveAvgPool2d → 2048维特征向量
3. 分类头:
   - Linear(2048→512) + BatchNorm1d + ReLU + Dropout(0.5)
   - Linear(512→256) + BatchNorm1d + ReLU + Dropout(0.3)
   - Linear(256→10)
